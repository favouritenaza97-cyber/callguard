What is real: the transcript scanner runs real, rule-based pattern matching in your browser — weighted categories plus combo bonuses, in three languages. Microphone input uses the browser's Web Speech API for live captions, and the waveform shows your actual microphone level while listening.

What is not built: acoustic deepfake detection. Telling a synthetic voice from a real one needs a trained audio model (e.g. trained on ASVspoof-style data) and is future-development scope, not faked here. A low score is not proof a call is safe, and a high score is a warning, not a verdict — always verify on a number you trust.
